Orbit This!

A lightweight 2D Newtonian orbital mechanics sandbox. Adjust your velocity vectors, use predicted trajectories, and maintain a stable orbit!

Altitude: 0 km
Speed: 0 km/s
Zoom: 1.0x (Scroll to Zoom)

Flight Controls:

  • W / S or Up / Down Arrow: Prograde / Retrograde Thrust (Tangential velocity)
  • A / D or Left / Right Arrow: Radial Thrust (Inward / Outward)
  • R Key: Reset Ship to initial position
  • Mouse Scroll: Zoom In / Out
