Collapsed

Navigate procedural mazes, press S or Down to drop through platforms, toggle shields, and unobserve reality with Q!

Level: 1
Hearts: 🔺🔺🔺
Shield: Off
Status: Find the Gate!

Controls (Keyboard & Mobile):

  • Move: A / D or Left / Right Arrow (or mobile ◀ ▶ buttons)
  • Jump: W, Space, Up Arrow (or mobile JUMP button)
  • Drop Through Platform: S Key or Down Arrow (or mobile DOWN button)
  • Shield: L Key (or mobile SHIELD button) to toggle ON / OFF
  • Quantum Unobserve: Q Key (or mobile Q button) to enter cloud mode, adjust with W/A/S/D or touch buttons, press Q again to collapse!
  • Reset: R Key
