Observe

Navigate to the gate, toggle your shield with L, and unobserve with Q to warp probability clouds through solid barriers!

Level: 1
Hearts: 🔺🔺🔺
Shield: Off
Status: Find the Gate!

Controls:

  • A / D or Left / Right Arrow: Move Left / Right
  • W, Space, or Up Arrow: Variable Jump (Hold for higher)
  • L Key: Toggle Energy Shield ON / OFF (1s active capacity, 2s cooldown)
  • Q Key: Toggle Unobserve / Observe (Warp cloud with W/A/S/D under constant area conservation, press Q again to collapse & rematerialize!)
  • R Key: Reset Level
